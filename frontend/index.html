Why Donate Blood?

A single donation can save up to three lives.

⏱

Every 2 Seconds

Someone in India needs blood. Accidents, surgeries, cancer and thalassemia patients depend on donors.

✅

Who Can Donate?

Healthy people aged 18–65, weighing at least 45 kg, can donate every 3 months (men) or 4 months (women).

💪

Safe & Simple

Donation takes about 10 minutes, uses sterile single-use kits, and your body replaces the blood within weeks.
